# Caravel 0.13.0

**ELASTIC P1.4.** Temporary, reversible spring movement of HOME controls, now with a **tempo-synced BOUNCE** (one full oscillation from 1/16 note to 64 bars) and an **endless RETURN (INF)**, plus a smaller factory SAMPLE set and a fix for the One-Core flash layout. **This release changes the HOME project format (HPM1 v5): older firmware may refuse a project saved by 0.13.0. Back up first.**
Previous release: [0.12.0](release-0120.md). Step-by-step guides with firmware screens from the matching emulator: [ELASTIC first steps](tutorials/elastic-first-steps.md), [linked springs](tutorials/elastic-linked-springs.md), [ambient recipes](tutorials/elastic-ambient-recipes.md) and the [tutorial overview](tutorials/README.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Newer FM-1 units (flash UC25HQ80, JEDEC ID `0xB36014`) are recognised** (since 0.12.0). Up to 0.11.0 only the Puya P25Q80 (`0x856014`) was accepted: on a newer unit projects stayed in RAM (`SAVED (RAM)`, gone after power-off) and the installer's update was not started. The ID list is unchanged in 0.13.0 (`firmware/src/project.c`, `flash_id_known`). Not tested by us on a real UC25HQ80 unit (host test of the ID list; one user's report for Felucca's 1.8.2 beta).

## What is new

- **ELASTIC (HOME ACTION, seventh bank).** ELASTIC lets a supported HOME control swing away from its value and come back like a spring. It adds a temporary value while the sound is rendered and does not change the stored parameter. The banks now run RANDOM, JOURNEY, HARM, STASIS, **ELASTIC**, PAIR, LIVE (C#5 one bank back, D#5 forward, as in 0.12.0).
  - **Perform:** A1 (D5) **ARM** or OFF for the selected track, A2 (E5) **PULSE**, A3 (F5) **HOLD / RESUME**, A4 (G5) **RESET** (outside SETUP). With SCOPE = CELL you ARM, turn a supported HOME card once to select it and again to excite it; with SCOPE = ROW the eligible cards of the visible HOME row move together. **The four tracks have independent arms and settings**; changing the selected track does not switch off a spring on another track. Arms, HOLD, phase and spring positions are not saved.
  - **SETUP (hold the bank key), page TIMES:** K1 **SCOPE** CELL / ROW, K2 **PULL** (about 16 ms to 45 s), K3 **RETURN** (a short glide up to 5 minutes, or **INF**), K4 **RANGE**. **In SETUP, A4 switches to the page PHYSICS** (and back to TIMES) instead of resetting: K1 **BOUNCE**, K2 **REFLECT** (0 to 100 %, default 70), K3 **COUPLE** (OFF to 100 %, ROW scope only), K4 **SEED** (0 to 255, a reproducible signed coupling pattern between the four ROW springs).
  - **BOUNCE is a musical period.** It is the length of one complete oscillation, in 11 steps: 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1/1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64 BAR (a bar is four quarter notes, the FM-1's transport grid). At 120 BPM that is 125 ms for 1/16 and 128 s for 64 BAR. The default is 1/4. The former 1/32 step is reserved and cannot be chosen or loaded. The period follows the **internal tempo or the tempo the firmware estimates from the external MIDI clock**; a tempo change retimes a running spring **without restarting its phase**. A gesture or PULSE starts at once with its own phase.
  - **RETURN INF:** the spring keeps its full amplitude until HOLD, RESET, OFF or until its target becomes invalid (for example an engine change). INF needs a tempo-synced BOUNCE: choosing it converts a project's old numeric BOUNCE to a synced one. No extra voice and no stored oscillator phase.
  - **HOME display:** while the ELASTIC bank is selected, eligible cards show a small spring mark next to the number; an armed card keeps its ARM border and dot. While a spring really moves (or a coupled ROW card moves), a six-link spring replaces the number; the value shows for half a second after you turn the card's encoder. HOLD freezes the spring and adds the pause mark. Other cards stay numeric.
  - **Which controls move:** a curated list only (continuous timbre knobs per engine, plus the track's FILTER, PAN, CHORUS / DELAY / REVERB sends, DIST, insert mix and the filter / shape modulation amounts); not pitch, tempo, levels, envelope times, modes, voices, ARP or SEQ. Anything else answers `NOT ELASTIC`. **ELASTIC cannot be MIDI-learned** (`ELASTIC: NO MIDI LEARN YET`).
- **Project format HPM1 v5.** Every project you save with 0.13.0 carries a HPM1 **version 5** HOME capsule, whether or not you use ELASTIC. It adds nine bytes per track (SCOPE, PULL, RETURN, RANGE, BOUNCE as two bytes, REFLECT, COUPLE, SEED). What 0.13.0 does when it loads:
  - **v5** and **v4** (the earlier ELASTIC test format): the nine bytes are checked first, and a capsule that fails the check is refused before anything changes. A v4 BOUNCE is a plain number (1 to 1000) and keeps its old behaviour until you change BOUNCE; a v5 BOUNCE is either such a number or a tagged musical period; INF is only accepted with a musical BOUNCE.
  - **v1, v2 and v3** (0.7.1 to 0.12.0): load as before, with ELASTIC at its defaults (SCOPE CELL, BOUNCE 1/4, REFLECT 70, COUPLE OFF, SEED 0).
  - After a load, COUPLE starts quiet and nothing is armed.
  - **0.12.0 reads only versions 1 to 3** (the header check in `experiments/home-perf/home_store_hw.c` of 0.12.0 accepts nothing else), so it does not accept a v5 capsule (older versions were not checked; no Caravel project opens in the official V15). **Upgrading is safe, going back is not** for projects saved by 0.13.0. Reinstalling older firmware does not turn a v5 project back into an older one.
  - The project format of the sequencer (FUN11, since 0.12.0) is unchanged.
- **Smaller factory SAMPLE set (27,014 B less flash).** PIANO now uses **three** full-length recordings (MIDI roots 36, 60, 84; before: five zones), FLUTE **two** (roots 72, 96; before: three), with the same 0.75 s sample, fade and FLUTE sustain loops. The generated sample data shrinks from 72,772 B to 45,758 B (`SMP_DATA`, production profile). The five set IDs, the aliases, the four presets and the three user slots are unchanged, and the separately exportable **PIANO HD** user sample keeps all five PIANO zones. GRAIN and SLICE use the retained middle-C PIANO recording.
  - **Some notes sound different.** Each recording now covers a wider key range and is transposed further, so timbre changes in some registers, and an upward-transposed one-shot can end earlier (no recording was shortened). Six host-render hashes changed on purpose (SAMPLE presets 00, 01, 03 and GRAIN GLITCH, FROZEN, SHIMMER); 141 of the 147 compared renders did not change.
  - **How this was judged:** the maintainer chose this variant after listening to host-rendered examples and called them "fein"; the maintainer also reports a successful device test of the RC1 package (see below, no per-item results). **Not a measured listening test on the final build.**
- **One-Core layout fits again.** At the previous ELASTIC state the one-core layout (`FELUCCA_CPU2=0`) failed to link (XIP region over by 292 B). The size policy that builds cold UI, store and editor functions as separate calls (`tools/size_fns.py`) now applies to it as well; the audio path is instruction-identical (`fm1_alnk0_irq` 310 instructions, RAM-code inner loop 925 instructions).
- **Fix (since 0.12.0): the MOD slot DST knob.** The list that orders the destinations of the MOD slots' DST knob lacked the last destination, **SCAN** (the STASIS read position), so turning a DST knob (MOD slots 1 to 4) to its right end read **one byte past the table** and an arbitrary neighbouring byte decided the value; SCAN could not be selected on the knob. This was in 0.12.0 and in RC1. The table has SCAN now, a compile-time check keeps the four ordered lists in step with their name lists, and a compile-time check does the same for the note-time list DTIME_ORDER. The change matters only for that DST knob at its right end; stored projects, MIDI and the editor protocol are not affected.
- 0.12.0's changes (HARMONY, LINK, the SCL pages, HOME ACTION keys, UC25HQ80 detection) are included.

## What changes for you

- **Back up before you update** (`tools/caravel_backup.py` or the installer's backup; see the limit on the backup tool below). Projects saved by 0.13.0 are not readable by 0.12.0 and earlier.
- **A new bank sits between STASIS and PAIR.** Tapping the bank keys takes one more step to get around. The key map is unchanged.
- **PIANO and FLUTE sound different in some registers**, and a project that plays SAMPLE PIANO or FLUTE, or GRAIN or SLICE from PIANO, sounds a little different.
- Nothing else changes unless you use ELASTIC.

## What it costs

These are the numbers of the **final 0.13.0 build**, measured on the release source with the JieLi SDK (`caravel-validate` container, three layouts). They are 4 B smaller than the RC1 numbers in every layout (RC1 default: 553,020 B): the version text is shorter and the MOD DST table is one byte longer, which together move the data by 4 B.

| Layout | App image | Free in the 581,564 B slot | Static RAM free (guard 4,096 B) | Pool free |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Default | 553,016 B | 28,548 B | 4,448 B | 10,168 B |
| One core | 552,192 B | 29,372 B | 6,388 B | 10,172 B |
| No RAM DSP | 552,776 B | 28,788 B | 4,448 B | 10,168 B |

- **Flash:** the default image is 25,488 B smaller than 0.12.0's (578,504 B); the sample change frees 27,014 B of data (about 27,088 B of image against the same P1.4 build without it, 580,104 B as measured for the RC), ELASTIC adds code. About 28 KB of the app slot are free (0.12.0: 3,060 B).
- **RAM** `.data+.bss`: 69,280 B (default) and 67,340 B (one core) of 73,728 B; the headroom stays above the 4,096 B guard in all three layouts. RAM is the thin margin: **352 B above the gate** in the default and no-RAM-DSP layouts (as at RC1), so a later feature has to be paid for in RAM.
- CPU on the device is **not measured**. The static budgets of the target loops pass and the RAM-code loop is 925 instructions without calls; these are link-time and host figures, not CPU use or interrupt deadlines.

## Known limits

- **BOUNCE is tempo-synchronous, not grid-locked.** A gesture or PULSE starts immediately; the spring does not snap to a sequencer or ARP step or a bar line and does not advance from the MIDI 24-PPQN pulses directly, only from the tempo estimate (internal tempo or the filtered external MIDI-clock estimate). The tempo used is limited to 20 to 300 BPM, and a UI stall longer than two seconds does not catch up. A running spring keeps its phase when the tempo changes.
- **What the clock checks cover:** an instrumented run of the actual firmware code on the host measured the published modulation at 30 to 300 BPM for 1/16, 1/4 and 1/1: the mean period error is below 0.07 % in all 21 cases (240 BPM, 1/16: 62.484 ms against 62.5 ms; 300 BPM: 50.000 ms). **Not covered:** long-duration drift with an external clock, STOP / START / CONTINUE behaviour on a device, whether a 1/16 spring at 240 to 300 BPM sounds smooth or stepped, and click-free behaviour at parameter boundaries.
- **ELASTIC** cannot be MIDI-learned; sequencer MOTION and step locks take priority over ELASTIC on a shared target; the list of eligible controls is a conservative pilot list (the source says "control-safe pilot, not hardware-auditioned"). COUPLE only acts on ROW scope.
- **Backup tools and v5 projects.** `tools/caravel_backup.py` of 0.13.0 saves and restores HPM1 v4 and v5 capsules byte-preserving (it does not interpret the capsule); copies of the tool from 0.12.0 or earlier stop with `Invalid HPM1 capsule` on such a device (the same kind of problem as in 0.9.0, see the [FAQ](faq.md)). This is tested on the host against synthetic v4 and v5 capsules and the firmware's USB handler code, **not against a device that holds v5 projects**. **The web backup (`web/fm1backup.js`) checks object sizes only and has not been verified on a device with v5 projects.**
- **Samples:** see "What is new". A project with SAMPLE, GRAIN or SLICE sounds different in some registers; the factory PIANO and the PIANO HD user sample are no longer bit-identical at notes where the factory set now picks a different recording.
- **CHORD EXT** is not in this release (parked). The limits of 0.12.0 (HARMONY, LATCH, HOME editor cells, DRONE and ENSEMBLE in the engine picker) were not re-examined for 0.13.0; read [0.12.0's limits](release-0120.md#known-limits).
- The shown order of the bank keys is the only navigation change; MIDI-learned controls of other banks keep working while ELASTIC is visible.

## Install

Back up first. Use the [web installer](https://bonszaimusic.github.io/caravel/webapp/installer/)
or download `Caravel-0.13.0-FM1-Testpaket.zip` from the
[release](https://github.com/bonszaimusic/caravel/releases/tag/v0.13.0). After reboot INFO must show `v0.13.0` (not `v0.13.0-rc1`).
Firmware: `caravel-0.13.0.fwsc`. Matching offline emulator: `Caravel-0.13.0-Emulator.html`. The release is built by the public release workflow; identical firmware files are expected from the reference build of this source: `caravel-0.13.0.fwsc` (610,074 B, SHA-256 `a1d560684519667b4051b35630380874065853a20a353a4f9d279c2bfee208b7`), `caravel-0.13.0-app.bin` (553,016 B), `caravel-0.13.0-loader.bin` (6,918 B), checked against the draft's `SHA256SUMS` before publication. See the [FAQ](faq.md) and the
[installation guide](installing.md) for USB discovery, OCT-/OCT+ and recovery. The firmware identity is `FM-1_999`, the same as every Caravel release since 0.3.4 (`tools/release_artifacts.py`), so the installer updates 0.12.0 in place.
Projects need a matching backup tool; keep a pre-update backup for going back.

## What is tested, and what is not

- **Host tests with the real firmware code, on the RC1 source** (code state `896119e`, run on 2026-11-13): the eight original host groups all passed with empty skip lists (including the AddressSanitizer / UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer runs, the all-engine dual-core parity and ThreadSanitizer on the host), the 28 ACTION workflow steps, 45 extra target-validation entries, 147 of 147 expected audio hashes in serial, one-core and threaded host configurations, the three layouts built with the actual JieLi SDK (above), the ABI / far-call / startup-copy suite (8 tests), the sample-zone policy, the matching browser build (seven-bank navigation, native parity, four-track arms, HPM1 v5 save / load and v1 migration), and an exact A/B of the factory samples (720 engine-switch cases: 576 changed on purpose, 144 unchanged; a run with the old samples reproduced all 720 old hashes).
  **Re-run on the final 0.13.0 source** (the release tree, one run on 2026-11-13 in the `caravel-validate` container, 12 min 17 s with four containers at a time): the 28 ACTION workflow steps and all eight host groups (UI and storage, synthesis and MIDI, sanitizers, storage and USB, effects, dual core, engines and samples, web and target) passed with empty skip lists; the ELASTIC P1.4 host test, the ELASTIC ownership gate, the sample-zone policy test and `tests/enum_order_test.c` under AddressSanitizer / UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer passed; the USB backup tests (the firmware's USB handler code with the Python client, and `web/test_backup.mjs`) passed; the three layouts and the package were rebuilt (numbers above). **Not re-run on the final source:** host ThreadSanitizer (it cannot run in the Apple-silicon container used for this run; the ThreadSanitizer result above belongs to the RC1 source), the HARM and CHORD EXT fuzz runs, and the jobs that only run on GitHub.
- **Reviews:** internally reviewed, **not independently reviewed**. The checks above were run by AI-assisted sessions of the same model family as the author; a human has not audited the source.
- **The final 0.13.0 is not byte-identical to the tested RC1.** It differs by the version text (`v0.13.0-rc1` to `v0.13.0`) and by the MOD DST knob table fix with its compile-time checks (a table of 30 instead of 29 entries; one compile-time assert for DTIME_ORDER). Nothing else in the firmware differs: a function-by-function comparison of the disassembly of all three layouts found only the shifted data addresses (4 B) and the one-byte longer table. The fix changes behaviour only for the MOD DST knob at its right end. A regression test for it (`tests/enum_order_test.c`, built under ASan in three configurations) comes with the fix, and it passed on the final source together with the re-run of all host groups (see above).
- **On hardware:** the 0.13.0-rc1 package was **tested successfully on an FM-1 by the maintainer (RC1, no individual results)** (statement of 2026-11-13: "Es ist auf der hardware erfolgreich getestet"). **No per-item results are recorded:** what was checked, how it sounded and read, with which clock and controller, and the hash of the package that was installed are not known to us. **The final build has not been installed on a device**, and the two differences from RC1 above have not been tested on one.
- **Not covered:** a protocolled hardware, listening, readability, clock and controller test (1/16 at 240 to 300 BPM, external MIDI clock with STOP / START, the 240 x 240 display, v5 SAVE / power-cycle), the CPU and interrupt deadlines on the device, the PIANO and FLUTE timbre by a recorded listening test of the final firmware, a real UC25HQ80 unit, ThreadSanitizer on a device (the host run simulates the two cores with threads), the backup tools (terminal and web) and the editor against a device holding v5 slots, and the publication-history audit of the source.
- **CI:** the private candidate's GitHub-hosted jobs failed without executing a step (the maintainer reports that the Actions budget is used up); all results above are local runs. The release workflow builds a draft and the maintainer publishes it only after its checks and the comparison of the draft's firmware files with the local reference build are green; their results are not part of this text.
